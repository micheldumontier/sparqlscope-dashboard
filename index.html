How each engine took each source in this run. A diverged engine stored the source differently from its canonical form (expected → stored counts); a failed one did not load it. "Union view" is the truth an engine is scored against: bag (a triple in two graphs counts twice) or set (the RDF merge), as the engine declares it merges graphs, by its union default graph or FROM. "Stores differently" is the datatype rewrites an engine was measured to make on what it stores: its datatype patterns cannot match truth, so its exact-count defects there are the engine's storage, not voidx's. They still count: exact means exact.

Estimate error by quantity

Relative error of estimates against the non-blank truth: median and 95th percentile over each run's estimates, then the median over repetitions. Exact counts have no error: any difference is a defect.

Estimators over every sample slice

Each corpus harvested 16 times at 1/16 and 16 times at 1/256, pinned to sampling, every estimate scored against its non-blank truth. 4b is what voidx reports (the conditional MLE for a pattern, exact 95 % bounds, clamped by the counts the run knows); 4b-uncapped is the same point and bounds before the caps, so the estimator's own bias shows apart from the caps' (where a cap equals the truth, capping biases the point low); pre-4b is h / π with ±2·RSE from the same hits. Bias and RMSE are of the relative error; coverage is the share of intervals holding the truth, with its range over slices; detection is the share of (true target, slice) pairs with an estimate. The size groups leave out literal objects.
